# Speed Types
## Pace
- Standard pace is AGI die
- Treated like the "currency" to pay to move around stuff
- ie. 1 pace is used to walk 1 meter on a clear ground

## Swimming
- 4 pace to swim 1 meter
- Without any special swimming traits, the creature is [[Vulnerable]] while swimming through liquid deeper than half their height

## Climbing
- 4 pace to climb 1 meter
- Without any special climbing traits, the creature is [[Vulnerable]] while climbing

## Jumping
- Jump distance is the larger of:
	- AGI dice / 2
	- VIT dice / 2
- Jumping movement uses up pace so can't jump further than your pace

## Phasing
- Available only with traits
- Movement through occupied spaces and structures
- If ending turn in an occupied space, takes 5 damage

## Flight
- Available only with traits
- Have to maintain the flight by moving every turn (can move around as per the move action)
	- Otherwise, starts gliding downwards

# Difficult Terrain
- Pace needed to travel through here is doubled

# Falling
- Damage determined by [[Size]]
- Can attempt a [[Attributes and Skills#Finesse]] check against the number of 2 m fallen to check still standing, otherwise [[Prone]]

# Mounted
- The mount is considered to be carrying the rider in their [[Inventory]] and speed is determined accordingly
