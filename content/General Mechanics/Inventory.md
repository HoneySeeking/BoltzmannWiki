- Slot based inventory as an abstraction over both size and weight
- Total slots you can carry = VIT dice (eg. VIT dice of d8 means you can carry 8 slots total)
- Larger creatures get more slots for the VIT as seen in [[Size]]
- **Encumberance**: Can carry things beyond VIT size, but each medium item above VIT size (round up small items) reduces AGI dice size by 1
- **Carrying other creatures**: Refer to [[Size]] for how many inventory slots they are and any gear they might be carrying
	- See also: [[Movement#Mounted]]

# Supplies 
- To simplify wilderness exploration, food, water, and other supplies will be abstracted as the medium item "Supplies"
	- Think of supplies existing in a super position of being any possible adventuring supply until the situation calls for a specific item, at which point, the super position collapses into that specific item
- Food enough to feed a raccoon sized creature for a day is single supplies
- Water enough to feed a raccoon sized creature for a day is single supplies
- Other adventuring supplies can be drawn from supplies, how many supplies should generally be determined by the size and the cost
