# Durations
- **Short Term** lasts Cd4 * 10 minutes irl
- **Long Term** lasts Cd4 hours irl
- **Indefinite**

# Maddnesses
1. [[Confused]]
2. Depression: decrease the size of all attribute dice by 1 per level of depression
3. Dissociation: you are perceiving yourself floating from behind your head, about 1 meter away. Disadvantage on all VIT and AGI checks
4. Must speak in rhymes or take 1d4 psychic Composure damage
5. Fall in love with the first thing you see
6. Incapable of understanding any spoken or written language
7. Extreme paranoia. Disadvantage on PRE checks
8. Believes to be dreaming and wants to wake up. Believes dreams to be the real reality.
9. Anatidaephobia: fear of a duck watching you at all times
10. Irrational fear of killing absolutely anything
11. The insatiable desire to kill absolutely anything
12. Capgras syndrome: believes party mates are replaced by doubles
13. Overwhelming urge to please others
14. Partial Amnesia: retains skills but forgets about everything before madness took place. Disadvantage on all INT checks
15. You believe you are a foreign spirit, merely possessing this body. You insist the “original owner” is still in here somewhere and sometimes try to let them take control
16. You compulsively narrate your thoughts and actions out loud, as if you’re being watched by an audience of unseen spirits who must be kept updated and entertained.
17. Alice in Wonderland syndrome: you cannot tell what size anything is. Roll for size for creatures every time you see them and roll for the size you perceive them as. Mechanically treat the size difference as being real for contested checks and like
18. Dark Forest: you have to survive no matter what. That probably means killing anything weaker before they get stronger.
19. Societal Disdain: you hate cities, towns, and civilization as a whole. Any interactions you make with people who are unfamiliar with the wilderness tend to be hostile or unhelpful
20. You occasionally see terrifying figures at the edge of your vision. They are never there when you directly look over. Whether these are you hallucinations or you're seeing something you shouldn't be able to is up to the BM.
