# Character Creation
You will be playing as a shamanic forest creature of roughly the size of a raccoon. Decide what species you wish to be, think about the answers to some of these questions:
- What does your clan look like?
	- What are traits and skills are valued?
	- How do they prepare for the winter?
	- How do they deal with predators?
	- If so, what craft/trade is your people known for?
- Are you exceptionally good at something? If so, how did that happen
- For longer adventures, please give me at least 2 NPCs to do stuff with
# Ego
- In the setting of Boltzmann, where reality exists the way that it does is because the world believes it to be so, Ego is a measure of how well differentiated and recognized by the world one is
- Analogous to XP or Levels in other systems, you start with 15 Ego

# Build
- Spend Ego on [[Attributes and Skills]]
- Pick [[Traits#Ancestral Traits]]
- Spend Ego on [[Traits#Learned Traits]]
- Calculate derived statistics:
	- [[Composure]]
	- [[Movement#Pace]]
- You start with the knowledge of [[Rituals#Channel]], [[Rituals#Totem Crafting]], and can start with one more for every ego you spend here

# Totems
- You start with two minor [[Totem|totems]] of some spirit of your choosing
	- What the totem looks like is up to you, but it has to be a physical piece of the spirit
	- eg. The wooden handle on your axe made from a tree spirit
	- eg. A flute made from the femur of an ancestor spirit
	- eg. Bowstring made from the sinew of a deer spirit
	- eg. A crystal charm from a cave spirit
- What this spirit is to you is up to you
- For each totem, pick any combination of [[Attributes and Skills]] upgrades, [[Traits|traits]], and [[Effects|effects]] (totaling up to 4 egos worth) that channeling this spirit grants you.
- You're allowed to pick magical traits
- Please don't pick something whose description seems to imply I'm still workshopping how this works, or help me flush it out if you want it
