# Attributes and Skills
Attributes and skills are measured in dice (d4, d6, ..., d12, d12+d4, d12+d6, ...). When attempting an attribute check, you roll the attribute dice and when attempting a skill check, the BM will decide the appropriate attribute to use with that skill for the particular check and you roll all the attribute and skill dice against a DC set by the BM.

For example, when attempting whittle a wooden charm, you might be asked for a Agility-Craftsmanship check, or when attempting to recall facts about the material used in the construction of something, you might be asked for an Intelligence-Craftsmanship check. During this check, your character with a d6 INT and d4 Craftsmanship might roll 3 and 4 on the dice respectively. The d4 explodes, rolling another d4, which rolls a 3, for a total of 10.

The ego needed to increase a die size of a particular attribute/skill gets an additional mulitplier for every d12. In other words, increasing an attribute d4 > d6 > d8 > d10 > d12 costs 1 ego, but then it starts costing two to d12 > d12+d4 > d12+d6... and then three for 2d12 > 2d12+d4...

# Attributes
All attributes start at d4. You can spend one ego to increase the die size by 1.
## Vitality
A measure of physical strength and resilience:
- Contributes to most damage
- Determines number of available [[Inventory#Slots]]
- Contributes to Composure Points, see [[Composure]]
- A character with a VIT d0 is dead

## Agility
A measure of finesse and speed:
- [[Movement#Pace]]
- A character with AGI d0 is [[Restrained]]

## Presence
A measure of ego, how well one's spirit projects its image onto the world, willpower, intuition, and charisma:
- Contributes to Composure Points, see [[Composure]]
- Contributes to some AoEs
- Used to resist [[Magic#Divergence Roll]]
- A character with a PRE d0 is dead

## Intelligence
Measure of memory, book smarts, raw cognitive acumen:
- Contributes to the range of most magical effects
- Can help find [[Magic#Local Spirits]] better
- A character with an INT d0 is comatose.

# General Skills
All skills start at d0 without a dice. When attempting something, the BM will pick the appropriate attribute and a skill for the task, and you roll both the attribute and skill dice against a DC set by the BM. If no attribute is specified, go with the default attribute. Spend one Ego here to get two die sizes to split up how you see fit.

| DC  | Difficulty                                               |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Trivial. Just about anyone can do it with minimal effort |
| 4   | Experienced people can do it consistently                |
| 8   | Challenging for experienced people                       |
| 12  | Almost impossible for most people to do consistently     |
| 16  | Challenging even for experts                             |

# Artisanal Skills
Skills needed to craft, repair, or appraise items. Can be used to reduce Ego Death during a [[Rest]]. Spend one Ego here to gain 2 die size increases to use here. If a skill involves creating objects, the same skill can **repair** or **appraise** them.

| Skill          | Covers                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Artcraft**   | Creative expression and ceremonial aesthetics.<br>- Music, dance, and performance<br>- Painting, ornamentation, ritual decoration<br>- Storytelling and oral tradition                                                    |
| **Brewcraft**  | Preparation and transformation of organic materials.<br>- Cooking and preservation<br>- Fermentation and brewing<br>- Herbal mixtures and medicines<br>- Poisons, dyes, and resins                                        |
| **Beastcraft** | Working with animals and animal materials.<br>- Herding and riding<br>- Training beasts<br>- Animal medicine<br>- Leather and bone working                                                                                |
| **Fibercraft** | Flexible materials and knot-based knowledge systems.<br>- Weaving and textiles<br>- Rope and braiding<br>- Quipu literacy and knot recording                                                                              |
| **Woodcraft**  | Shaping wood into tools and structures.<br>- Carving and joinery<br>- Bows and weapon shafts<br>- Tools and wooden structures                                                                                             |
| **Earthcraft** | Working with stone, clay, and mineral materials.<br>- Pottery and ceramics<br>- Masonry and stone construction<br>- Glassmaking and kilns<br>- Stone tools and weapon heads<br>- Mining, quarrying, and mineral appraisal |
