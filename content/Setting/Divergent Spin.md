# Divergent Spin
> Unbeknownst to most creatures of Boltzmann, the world itself seemingly randomly chooses spirits, whose beliefs to give a greater weight to. The spirit the world picks can be anything ranging from individuals, to the flavor of a specific soup, or the concept of Toralization. Most such [[Divergent God|Divergent Gods]] go their whole life without ever realizing they are one, as the few odd things they misremember causes the world itself to shape itself around their beliefs. 

All player characters are unknowingly Divergent Gods and as such, every session they get a single **Divergent Spin**, allowing them to spin their own narrative into the world. The BM might choose to award more Divergent Spins when you roleplay your character out in a compelling way or impress them in other ways.

Examples of what kind of narratives they are allowed to spin are provided below, although the BM has a final say over what can go. You should not be allowed to retcon established lore, but allowed to add onto it.


# Facts
You can establish truths about places and phenomena in the world. You may know that a certain location is renowned for its high quality flint for knapping, or that a band of mischievous fairies operate in the area. The BM judges what is appropriate, and what is reasonable for you to know.

# Contacts
When coming to an area you have been to before, you can create an NPC (spirit or otherwise) who is situated in the area, and who you have met before. The BM decides how they have changed since you last met, what they think of you now, and their current predicament. eg. "I remember my grandmother saying this river used to have a playful spirit. Maybe it still does, if we approach it with the right offering."

Alternatively, you can decide that you already know a certain NPC, and that your relationship is a positive one. The BM may disallow it, if the contact would make the adventure less fun.

# Events
Players can define minor ongoing events in the world that are happening in the background. This could be a local festival, an economic boom, or a minor magical phenomenon. eg. "There’s been a mysterious blue flame flickering on the cliffs for a week now. Some say it’s a sign of conflict".

# Flashbacks
So long as it makes sense in the fiction, you can have small flashbacks to a time where you prepared something like planting a trap there or stashing something there

# Rolls
All the dice involved roll can be (immediately after rolling) set to the max, such that you get one guaranteed explosion.
