
# Fox Speech
The common spoken language of most mammalian creatures and most other creatures who can speak. The language of the bears have been spread as the lingua franca of the entire world. Canonically sounds and feels like [Toki Pona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toki_Pona).

# Pheromones
In the forest full of critters, in the neolithic world before writing, pheromones largely replace its niche. Almost all creatures are capable of secreting pheromones to communicate basic ideas through olfactory means, such as alarm, trail, food, and sex. Pheromone markers can be left to persist in an area, such as marking territory through urine, but this will get washed away during any precipitation.

# Webspeak
Language of the spiders and other bugs. Transmitted not through speech but through vibrating the medium you're standing on. Malleable objects like a spiderweb or thing bits of flora can express full extent of ideas but the language is limited to simple ideas like "threat", "food", and directions on solid ground.

# Quipu
The written language of the spiders, adapted by the vertebrates to other cordage mediums. Each different type of knot contains different semantic information, and quipu books look like a central loop with many long ropes with knots coming out of them.

# Blade Script
Adoption of Quipu into a written medium, consisting of continuous connected flows. Looks like [Mongolian Script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_script).
