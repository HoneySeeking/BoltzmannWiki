# Overview
Welcome to the world of Boltzmann (new and improved!).

Animism is the main attraction of the setting, where spirits permeate *EVERYTHING* in the world, from the largest mountain to the smallest pebble. Gods in this world are just the most powerful spirits, who tend to be spirits of large natural features like mountains, lakes, rivers, the sky, the world, though only the more localized spirits tend to be concentrated enough to actually interact with little mortals. As the world draws associations between things their spirits start fusing, in an ever ceasing march from animism to polytheism to monotheism, at which point, the dreams of this singular universal spirit leads to pantheism and animism and the cycle repeats.

Successorship is a big theme of the world, and ancestor veneration is nigh universal and basically all the religious figures were once living breathing individuals. Such spirits might stick around places and items important to them in life, becoming associated with it and potentially fusing with it, becoming the interface to the land or the genius loci of it itself. Items passed down or crafted from your fore bearers link you to them, allowing for shamans to channel their attributes. Daily life in such a world tend to involve countless little rituals showing respect to the spirits, which usually take seconds to minutes.

The laws of reality in any given space is defined by powerful spirits in the area, with truly universal spirits defining a baseline reality, and any truly wild area is rife with spirits who add and remove rules to existing foreign to us around them. Building civilization in this world is the process of pacifying the more dangerous wild spirits, and building your own national spirits who enforce a baseline reality for its inhabitants. Moreover, almost all the natural features like mountains and woods and lakes have an associated animal spirit and their movement sees these features also moving with them at varying speeds. Cartography becomes exceptionally difficult but that much more important as you don't want to be the one city that didn't realize the mountain was walking over to you. Thus vast majority of people are semi-nomadic or nomadic, often building moving homes.

# Setting
The woodland creatures we are playing-as make their living and building their societies in the endless dark woods of the [[Canopy]], whose roots tie together the many flying islands of [[Mars]] into the two landmasses [[Phobos]] and [[Deimos]]. [[Mars]] itself and the 6 other planets orbit the [[Sun]], the swarm of phoenixes in a perpetual flight downwards, which in this looping world, gives us the day-night cycles. The space between the islands and the planets is the great [[Expanse]], dominated by storms and winds, where the nomadic clans herd whales and dolphins.

As the blizzard-tentacles of the winter squid from the umbral side of [[Phobos]] extends its reach ever further and further antumbra, the reptilian nations built their temple cities around ruins and artifacts of their forebearers, trying to decipher the mystery of their abrupt disappearance. In their single minded focus, they enraged the many natural spirits exploited for their industry and diverging interpretations of the great mystery lead to disunity. It was in this turmoil, a Tiger clan formed the [[Stone Sun Nation]], confederating clans of Raven, Owl, Bear, Wolf, Turtle, Fox, and Beaver, conquering most of [[Phobos]] with the aid of the discontent natural spirits, pushing the surviving reptiles into [[Deimos]]. Before the completion of the conquest, the tigers fell into infighting, and their successors have not yet been able to unify the great clans to rival the glory of the original [[Stone Sun Nation]]. 

It has been about 3 centuries since then and the fractious land of the [[Canopy]] now sees many smaller nations titling themselves the rightful successors to the [[Stone Sun Nation]] both for the unrivaled glory of completing the conquests and authority over the spirits pacified and/or loyal to the original nation. As a result, the responsibilities for the original 8 clans in society now serves as the blueprints for arranging your society today, even if each individual modern nation may only contain a subset of the original 8, with all other peoples being seen as a lower caste of generalist labor, food, or sacrfiices. Below is a general description of the clans.

## Clans
### Bear Clan
The islands and the mountains are seen as large hibernating bears, and it is the bear's duty to maintain the natural balance. Brown bears accomplish this by ruthlessly eating anything that harms the balance. Also responsible for pacifying hostile spirits of the land such that the other clans can settle around here or to earn the service of these spirits. Usually done through sacrificially sacrificing their own shamans to posses some feature of the land. Knows the plants of the land well both for healing, eating, and harming purposes.

### Wolf Clan
Pathfinders and rangers. Responsible for maintaining roads and being the ones to venture into the endless and unknowable canopy. Responsible for herding the livestock in pastoralist communities. Detectives and trackers. Responsible for passing messages across communities. Skirmishers and scouts in combat. Associated with mounted archery.

### Turtle Clan
Lore and culture keepers. Long lifespan to learn all the stories and recount them to the children and those who need council. Maintains the turtle houses lots of communities in the canopy live in. Done by sacrificing their shamans to posses the turtle shaped wooden houses they build that resemble large turtles, where the main shell is the living spaces. The houses can walk around, animated by the spirit of the turtle shamans/elders. 

### Tiger clan
As the rulers of the original stone sun nation, associated with nobility and regality. Absolutely refuses to have anyone else above them in the hierarchy. Solitary, assassins, secret police/enforcers of their law. Propaganda. Strategic decision making. Often found as the rulers of polities by clawing their way up there. Prone to infighting. However not all tribes with a tiger clan is ruled by their tiger clan (yet).

### Beaver clan
Agriculture and industry. Construction of borders through irrigation and moving the rivers and walls, colonization of new lands, turning pacified land into livable lands, controls the rivers and acts as power brokers.

### Owl clan
Keepers of forbidden knowledge, pushing the limits of shamanism, channeling ever more dangerous and powerful spirits. Largely feared and disgraced.

### Raven clan
Keepers of scientific knowledge, pushing the limits of technological development. Hoarding shiny things and shiny knowledge. Discovery. Middle management, birds eye view over large projects.

### Fox clan
Working in boundaries, diplomats, espionage, deception, intelligence and counter intelligence, propaganda and narrative control.
