> Some loose classification of spirits you might encounter in the wild:
> - **Wild Spirits**: more like waves of phenomenon, where the very nature, the woods, and roots and rocks seem to grow alert and awake and alien in its reasoning. Occasionally has an avatar that's some wild animal that died in its vicinity, doing the warden ritual unintentionally.
> - **Warden Spirits**: people sacrificed to become faces of wild spirits, tied to some natural features/phenomenon. Acts as a more convenient interface between the people and the wild spirit, and has a small influence over the powers of the wild spirit.
> - **Feral Spirits**: warden spirit who has become so fused with the wild spirit they represent that they have become just as alien in their mind. Usually happened as a result of the people who maintained them no longer being around.

# Totem
- Totems are items associated with the spirit and provide channels to commune with and channel said spirits
- Possession is potentially bottlenecked by a small totem when channeling large spirits, ie. a minor totem of the [[Stone Sun Nation]] is going to be of a similar magnitude to a minor totem of your grandfather
- If a shaman dies while channeling through a totem, the totem is irreversibly destroyed (and the associated spirit is damaged on a metaphysical level)
- A single totem can be used for only a single possession at a time
- You will likely start with a few totems, but see [[Rituals#Totem Crafting]]
