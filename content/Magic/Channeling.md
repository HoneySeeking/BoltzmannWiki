

# Channeling
- Unless specified otherwise, you can channel any spirit given you have its totem
- The ego death associated with the totem you're using can be reduced by several things as shown in the table below (to a minimum of 1), but generally, channeling is easier if the world considers you to be the spirit you're channeling or its successor
- After these reductions, increase your [[Ego Death#Bound]] by this value then a [[Divergence Roll]]
- Channeling a spirit takes a 10 * ego death minutes ritual
- Ending a possession takes ritual of equal duration as the channeling but requires no [[#Divergence Roll]]

| Relationship with spirit                       | Ego Death Modifier |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Next of kin                                    | -1                 |
| Physical resemblance/imitation                 | -1                 |
| The spirit is willing                          | -1                 |
| You already have a trait the possession grants | -cost of trait     |
| [[Spiritmark]] from this spirit                | -# of spirit marks |
