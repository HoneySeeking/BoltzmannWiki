## Minor Spirit Offerings
d12:
1. Burn fragrant herbs or incense. Spirits enjoy the scent, particularly those of the wind, hearth, or sky.
2. Imitate the spirit. Add something to your appearance or behavior that mimics the appearance of the spirit or how you interpret the spirit as looking.
3. Burn a piece of meat, fish, or bread as a meal for the spirit.
4. Make a painting, textile, or a quipu depicting it, preferably somewhere it will last. Beat 4d6 on a Knotting, Weaving, or Painting check.
5. Carve a representation of the spirit into wood or stone and leave it somewhere meaningful. Beat 4d6 on a Woodcarving or Masonry check.
6. Compose a song or a speech in the spirit's name and perform it in public. Beat 4d6 on a Performance or an instrument check. 
7. Let the spirit witness a secret. Speak something deeply personal aloud in its (totem's) presence. It will hold your words, whether as a confidant or blackmailer.
8. Leave/Scatter/Bury something valuable to you by the spirit's physical presence.
9. Hunt and offer a small animal to the spirit. Some spirits may require it be cooked, others raw, and some demand the first bite but all demand it be fresh.
10. Spread your own blood on a site or an item important to the spirit such as its totem, gaining 1 [[Wound]].
11. The spirit unusually hungry today; roll twice on this table.
12. The spirit unusually hungry today; roll on the Major Spirit Offering Table.
