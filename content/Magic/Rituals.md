## Totem Crafting
**Deity**: [[Convergence]]
**Duration**: 1 hour + craft duration
**When**: Anytime
**Where**: Any shrine/temple
You bring some body part or item significant to someone who is dead to a place of spiritual significance and work to turn that item into something artistic during the course of the ritual as a burial ritual. Roll the appropriate [[Attributes and Skills#Artisanal Skills]] roll. You then roll a 1D20, add all the relevant modifiers from the table below, and if you beat 25 on that roll, the BM determines the [[Traits]] and/or the [[Effects]] this totem provides and its other properties. The BM would be happy if you gave him hints as to what kinds of traits you're looking for here through what parts of the deceased you turn into the totem and what the totem actually is. Afterwards, you can treat it as a normal [[Magic#Totem]]

| Condition                                                          | Modifier          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------- |
| Result of artisinal skill                                          | 1/4th of the roll |
| The deceased is associated with the ritual site                    | +1/+2/+3          |
| You killed the deceased                                            | +2                |
| The deceased liked you                                             | +1/+2/+3          |
| Most of the deceased is being used to craft the totem              | +2                |
| The deceased has been dead for longer than a day/month/season/year | -1/-3/-5/-10      |
| Moon is new/full                                                   | -2/+2             |

## Commune
**Deity**: #todo wolf
**Duration**: 1 hours
**When**: Anytime
**Where**: Place of significance to the spirit\*
**Supplies**: A totem of the spirit being communed with\*. Small dose of entheogen. Offerings of incense and food.
\* Only one of them is necessary
The shaman enters a trance and asks questions from the target spirit. Every question has 1 in 6 chance of ending the ritual after the answer. How much of the answer you understand depends on the size of the spirit. You can understand whole sentences of information from minor spirits, short phrases from major spirits, either "convergence" or "divergence" from minor deities, and ?????? from major deities. Depending on the spirit, they might not give up the information you seek for free. 

Moreover, everyday objects can be communed with, but requires a bit more effort on your side push their anima communable levels. Roll on the [[Offering Tables#Minor Spirit Offerings]] to determine appropriate way to do this. Then, they can answer questions such as about previous users or place of origin, but its memories of before being animated is foggy and dreamlike. With another [[Offering Tables#Minor Spirit Offerings]], you gain the [[Fetishize]] and [[Animate]] effects to use on this item.

**Learned by** by assisting in the ritual every day for a full lunar month.

- think divination, commune, contact other plane, speak with dead, control weather, sending, locate...

## Corvid Conspiracy
**Deity**: [[Iron Magpie]]
**Duration**: 1 hour
**When**: Anytime
**Where**: Anywhere accessible by birds
**Supplies**: Small dose of entheogen. Offerings of incense and food. Shiny offering for the bird depending on request
You request help from the great kleptomaniac god of corvids, knowledge, and shiny things, the [[Iron Magpie]], who send one of its agents to help you out. The bird can do bird things such as deliver a message, an item, act as extra pairs of eyes in the sky, but will not enter combat for you. The bird themselves expect payment for their services.
**Learned by** making a pilgrimage to the [[Iron Grove]] and leaving an offering of something shiny or of information the [[Iron Magpie]] did not know about before
- think animal messenger, Speedy Courier, sending, locate

## War Performance
**Deity**: #todo badger
**Duration**: 10 minutes
**When**: Anytime
**Where**: Anywhere
**Supplies**: Large dose of entheogens. Musical instrument that is a totem
You start a performance that requires two actions to maintain every turn lasting for the duration of the ritual. You roll the skill dice for the instrument (without associated attribute dice) and all creatures allied to you can choose to be composed for that much at the start of their turn if they can hear your performance. You must choose only one if there are multiple such performers.
**Learned by** performing alongside a war performer with at least a d6 on their skill with their instrument on at least a small dose of entheogens and witnessing a moment where their performance die explodes at least twice in the same roll.

## Resurrect
**Duration**: 12 hours
**When**: Full moon
**Where**: The place of death
**Supplies**: Heroic dose of entheogens for all participants, completely healed body of the deceased, totems associated with the deceased
All participants enter a trance, and goes through each of the totems across across the 4 layers of the Wheel/Circular Tree. The longer someone has died, the longer their ego has diffused into the world, and the more of their ego the spirits they were associated with in life now hold. It is up to the shaman and his party to convince these spirits that the deceased's time is not yet up and that they're still needed in here and now. Some spirits may want something in return, and some spirits may not want to let go at all. Any ego not released, on top of reducing the deceased's max ego also becomes permanent ego death. After rebuilding the individual, the deceased themselves needs to be convinced to come back to their body. After experiencing consciousness purely as a spirit, aware of the mystical connections bonding everything, their sense of self may have changed substantially.
- Think [Corridor of Dreams](https://berserk.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_349_(Manga))
- [[Rot]] ritual
**Learned by** reaching 32 ego death at some point and having helped in the ritual within the same year.
