- [[Ego Death]]: a measure of how connected you are to the world and the spirits in it; how great of a spiritual conduit you are
- [[Totem]]: crafting totems from fallen friends and foes
- [[Channeling]]: using totems to gain the attributes and traits from spirits
- [[Spells]]: building and casting the magical effects gained from channeling
- [[Divergence Roll]]: magical mishaps
- [[Entheogens]]: essential for rituals and helpful for spells
