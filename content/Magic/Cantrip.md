# Cantrips
- Channeling a spirit is typically fraught with dangers and typically a torrent that's hard to control once let loose
- Most cases, it is impossible to channel them just a little bit
- But when you get marked, the nature of the spirit becomes your nature a little bit, thus allowing for smaller channelings
- Such smaller channelings are infinitely spammable without needing a Divergence Roll and takes the form of non-damaging smaller magnitude versions of the effects gained from local spirits
	- eg. [[Freeze]] to cool your tea
	- eg. [[Fire]] to light a campfire
