# Divergence Roll
- Channeling the awesome powers of the spirits of the world is not a riskless endeavor
- When you open up your soul for magic, hostile spirits might try to force their way in, partially possessing you
- Roll a PRE check against your ego death
- If your Ego Death is smaller or equal to your PRE die, the effect automatically succeeds, but if you fail the check, roll on the [[#Divergence]] table for the resulting effect
- If your Ego Death is larger than your PRE die, then you must succeed on this roll for the effect to succeed
- If your Ego Death is larger than your Ego, roll on the [[#Dissonance]] table for failed effects instead

# Dissonance
1. Roll twice more on the table
2. Ego flows out into the world. Lose 1D4 ego.
3. Roll on the [[Madness]] table for a long term madness
4. Roll on the [[Madness]] table for an indefinite madness
5. [[Blinded]] for Cd6 days
6. [[Bleeding]] for Cd12
7. [[Burning]] for Cd12
8. [[Confused]] for Cd6 days
9. [[Dazed]] for Cd6 days
10. [[Deafened]] for Cd6 days
11. [[Frightened]] of a random creature near for Cd6 days
12. [[Hastened]] for Cd6 days
13. [[Poisoned]] for Cd6 days
14. [[Silenced]] for Cd6 days
15. [[Slowed]] for Cd6 days
16. Your start turning into some other substance from the extremeties (roll for body part). 1D6:
	1. Random igneous rock
	2. Random sedimentary rock
	3. Random mineral
	4. Random metal
	5. Glass
	6. Bone
17. A random [[Effects]] is cast on you with 3D4 levels and as an aura with 3D6 meter radius affecting you
18. A random minor spirit attaches itself to you. Every hour 1D4 of your ego goes into growing a tumor in a random [[Body Parts#Table]] unless that part is chopped off. The chopped off tumor grows into a troll.
19. The spirit flows through you violenty. The spell succeeds, multiply one number in the spell by 4 and gain 2 levels of [[Exhaustion]]. Just gain the exhaustion if not casting
20. Ego flows in you. Gain 1D4 ego.
