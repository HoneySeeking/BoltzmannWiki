# Armor
- Each point of armor provides a flat damage reduction, except for:
	- Fall damage
	- Necrotic damage
	- Psychic damage
- You can't benefit from more than 2 shields at a time

| Type   | Example            | Armor | Minimum Vitality | Inventory Slots |
| ------ | ------------------ | ----- | ---------------- | --------------- |
| Shield | Shield             | 2     | d4               | Medium          |
| Light  | Leather            | 1     | d4               | Medium          |
| Medium | Wood               | 3     | d8               | Large           |
| Heavy  | Bone/Chitin/Bronze | 5     | d12              | Large           |
