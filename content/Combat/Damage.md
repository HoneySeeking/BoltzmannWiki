# Damage
- Unless specified otherwise damage goes through composure then vitality

# Composure Damage
- If something is specified to be a "Composure Damage" then it only composure and not Vitality

# Damage Multipliers
- **Immune**. takes no damage from that damage type
- **Resistance**. halves that kind of damage. applied after armor
- **Weak**. doubles that kind of damage. applied after armor

# Damage Type
- Blunt: clubs, axes, strong vibrations
- Rending: shlashy, stabby
- Energy: fire, force, radiant, lightning, cold
- Necrotic: poison, acid
