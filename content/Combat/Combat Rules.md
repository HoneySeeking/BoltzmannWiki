# Initiative
- No initiative
- Whoever is ready to go goes first
- From there, the turns are taken clockwise around the table
- The BM weaves in the NPCs into the initiative order as he sees fit, but most likely just gonna let all the players go then do all the NPCs

# Turn
- A complete cycle of everyone's turns is a **round**, spanning 6 seconds in game
- 3 actions per turn and all unused action at the end of your turn can be used as a reaction, and all actions are replenished at the **end of your turn**
## Actions
Beyond what's listed below, what's constitutes an action is subjective and generally up to the BM, but each action takes about 2-4 seconds. 
### Attack
- Each consecutive attack in the same turn gives an additional disadvantage on the attack
#### Weapon Attack
- Takes 1 action
- Damage is [[Attributes and Skills#Weapons Skills|Weapon Proficiency Dice]] + [[Weapons|Weapon Damage Dice]]
- Attacks just hit
- Attacking an unseen target gives disadvantage (but you need to know for sure where they are generally to attack (eg. obscured by fog but you know where in the fog they are))
- Attacking from stealth gives advantage
- Attacking someone not on guard (eg. sneak attack) bypasses their composure entirely

#### Grapple Attack
- Takes 1 action
- Contested Vitality-Athletics vs Vitality-Athletics or Agility-Finesse check
- Grappled creature is can attempt to break out for the grapple 2 actions, retrying the contest

| Size Difference         | Movement                                       |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Grappler is larger      | Grappler controls movement                     |
| Same Size               | Grappler controls movement and speed is halved |
| Grappled is larger      | Grappled controls movement and speed is halved |
| Grappled is much larger | Grappled controls movement                     |
#### Shove Attack
- Takes 1 action
- Contested Vitality-Athletics vs Vitality-Athletics/Agility-Finesse check
- Shove them VIT dice / 4 meter distance away and they fall prone

### Move
- 1 action
- Move up to your pace
- Movement can be broken up with other actions being done in the middle of the move action
#### Disengage
- 1 action
- Move half your pace without triggering exploit
#### Fall Prone
- 0 action
- Become [[Prone|Prone]]

### Hide
- 1 actions
- Can only be attempted when unperceived
- Contested Stealth check against whoever you're hiding from's Perception

### Prepare
- 2 actions to prepare 1 action action
- 3 actions to prepare 2 action action
- and so on
- Define a trigger for prepared action
- Can then be done as a release reaction 

### Draw
- 1 action to draw something from easy to reach places like a sheathe or over shoulders
- 3 actions to draw from packs and sacks

### Spellcasting
- See [[Magic/Spells|Spells]]
- 1 action to cast, then 1 action every turn to maintain any ongoing effects

## Reactions
### Exploit
- 1 action
- Use the moment of distraction as the opponent moves in/out your melee range to [[#Attack]] them or immediately after they attack you
- Can be weapon attack, grapple, or shove

### Interpose
- 1 action
- If an ally within 2 meters would be struck by an attack, you can push them out of the way and become the new target of the attack

### Release
- 0 action
- Perform prepared action when criteria is satisfied
### Resist
- 1 action
- Attribute check of the appropriate attribute to resist an effect not happening on your turn
- Dodge from an attack to reduce the damage by an agility check
- eg. Dodge out of the way of a falling tree, brace yourself mentally against magical effects
